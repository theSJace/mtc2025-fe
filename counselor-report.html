Recommended Strategies for Parents

Emotion Labeling Practice

Help Li Wei identify and label his emotions throughout the day. Use a feelings chart with facial expressions to make this concrete. Ask questions like "How are you feeling right now?" and validate his responses in both English and Mandarin.

Structured Social Interactions

Arrange playdates with one or two classmates in a comfortable environment like East Coast Park or Gardens by the Bay Children's Garden. Start with short, activity-focused interactions (30-45 minutes) and gradually increase duration as comfort grows.

Predictable Routines

Maintain consistent daily routines to reduce anxiety. Use visual schedules at home to help Li Wei understand what to expect. Give advance notice before transitions between activities, especially during busy weekends and exam periods.

Mindfulness Activities

Practice simple mindfulness exercises together for 5 minutes daily. Focus on breathing, body awareness, or sensory experiences. The "MindChamps Mindfulness" app has age-appropriate guided sessions designed for Singapore students.
