Key Features

/profile Command (Individual Chat)

Users set up their profile with nickname, occupation, hobbies, food preferences, and allergies. This information is used in group settings to provide personalized recommendations.

/makan Command (Group Chat)

Suggests food options and dining venues based on each family member's stored preferences and allergies. Considers everyone's dietary restrictions to recommend suitable options for family meals.

/jomxz Command (Group Chat)

Recommends family activities and outings based on each member's hobbies and interests. Provides options that align with multiple family members' preferences.

/sembang Command (Group Chat)

Sets up scheduled family reflection time. Families can choose frequency, day, and time for regular check-ins. The bot prompts reflection questions to facilitate family discussions and bonding.

Future Improvements

/game Command (Group Chat)

KeluargaAI can facilitate family game time by prompting turn-based games such as Wordle, teka teki, quizzes etc.

/workshops Command (Group Chat)

Suggests upcoming and ongoing workshops for families to attend. The bot can also provide bite-sized information of what the workshop entails.
